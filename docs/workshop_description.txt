[h1]Extended Rarity — new EPIC and MYTHIC rarity tiers[/h1]

[b]This is not a regular Workshop mod![/b] Extended Rarity is a BepInEx (DLL) mod and cannot be distributed through the Workshop. This page is a signpost.

[h2]Download & install instructions:[/h2]
https://github.com/YOUR_GITHUB/ExtendedRarity

[h2]What it does[/h2]
Adds two new rarity tiers on top of Common / Uncommon / Rare:
[list]
[*][b]EPIC[/b] — red
[*][b]MYTHIC[/b] — purple
[/list]
Paint any vehicle, turret or weapon with the new tiers in the loadout menu (hotkeys 5 and 6), and they will spawn in battle as ultra-rare finds. Colors, labels and spawn chances are fully configurable.

[h2]Requirements[/h2]
[list]
[*]BepInEx 5.4.23.x (x64) — [b]not[/b] BepInEx 6
[/list]

Works alongside all regular Workshop mutators and content mods. Source code is available on GitHub under the MIT license.
